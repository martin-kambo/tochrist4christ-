Mentorship

TC4C is building a space where members walk alongside one another as they grow in Christ.

Please note: mentors at TC4C are fellow believers offering encouragement, prayer, and companionship. Being a mentor does not make someone a counsellor, therapist, or pastor, and TC4C mentorship is not a substitute for professional mental-health care. This application is not a counselling form — please don't share medical or mental-health history, or private information about other people. Applications are reviewed by TC4C administrators.
